# Vendora Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## 1. How do I pay for my order?
We support multiple payment methods:
- Cash on Delivery (COD) (Default and most popular)
- Easypaisa
- JazzCash
- SadaPay

## 2. Is my payment secure?
Yes. For prepaid orders, payments are held securely by Vendora and only released to the vendor once the buyer confirms successful delivery.

## 3. How do I become a seller on Vendora?
You can sign up as a Vendor from the homepage. You will need to provide your business name, contact details, and upload a copy of your National ID (CNIC). Admin approval is required before your shop goes live.

## 4. What if I receive a damaged product?
Contact support@vendora.pk immediately with photos of the damaged item and the packaging. We will assist you in getting a replacement or a full refund under our Return Policy.

## 5. Can I cancel my order?
Orders can only be cancelled while they are in the "pending" status. Once an order is "confirmed" or "shipped", cancellation is no longer possible.
