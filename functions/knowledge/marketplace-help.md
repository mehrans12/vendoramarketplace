# Marketplace Help

Welcome to the Vendora Marketplace Help Center.

## How to use the AI Assistant
Vendora provides a smart AI chatbot in the bottom right corner of the screen. You can ask it to:
- Find specific products (e.g., "Find me a blue pottery vase under 5000").
- Answer questions about shipping or returns.
- Check the inventory of a specific item.

## Account Management
- You can update your profile information in the "Profile" section.
- If you forget your password, you can reset it via the Firebase Auth login screen using the "Forgot Password" link.

## Contacting Support
If the AI chatbot cannot resolve your issue, you can reach out to our human support team at support@vendora.pk. Our typical response time is 24 hours.
