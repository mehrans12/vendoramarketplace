# Buyer Policy

As a buyer on Vendora Marketplace, you are protected by our community guidelines and buyer protection program.

## Buyer Rights
- You have the right to receive products exactly as they are described on the listing.
- You have the right to secure and encrypted payments.
- You have the right to leave honest reviews (1 to 5 stars) after a verified purchase.

## Buyer Responsibilities
- Do not engage in abusive behavior or use inappropriate language in reviews or chatbot interactions.
- Ensure your shipping address is accurate before placing an order.
- Promptly report any suspicious vendor activity to Vendora Admin.

## Dispute Resolution
- In the event of a dispute, Vendora acts as a neutral mediator. Both the buyer and seller will be required to submit photographic evidence.
