# Vendora Shipping Policy

## Delivery Time
- Standard delivery across major cities in Pakistan (Karachi, Lahore, Rawalpindi, Islamabad) takes 3 to 5 business days.
- For remote areas and smaller cities, delivery may take up to 7 business days.

## Shipping Fees
- Vendora charges a flat shipping rate of Rs. 250 per package from a single vendor.
- If an order contains items from multiple vendors, each vendor's package will incur a separate Rs. 250 shipping fee, as items are dispatched directly from the respective merchants.

## Order Tracking
- Once an order is marked as "shipped" by the vendor, buyers can view tracking updates in the "My Orders" section of their profile.
