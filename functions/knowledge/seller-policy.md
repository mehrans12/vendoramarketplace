# Seller Policy

Vendora Marketplace relies on reliable and honest merchants. By registering as a vendor, you agree to these terms.

## Vendor Registration and Verification
- All vendors must submit a valid CNIC for identity verification.
- Stores will remain in "pending" status until an Admin manually approves the account.

## Product Listing Guidelines
- Products must be described accurately. Misleading titles or stock images of different products are strictly prohibited.
- Vendors must maintain accurate stock counts. Selling items that are out of stock will negatively impact your vendor rating.
- Selling illegal, counterfeit, or prohibited items (such as weapons, narcotics, or unauthorized pharmaceutical drugs) will result in immediate permanent account suspension.

## Fees and Payouts
- Vendora takes a 5% commission on all successful sales.
- Payouts are dispersed every Friday to the vendor's linked bank account or mobile wallet.
