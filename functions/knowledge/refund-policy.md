# Refund Policy

Vendora Marketplace strives to ensure that you get a full refund if your experience is unsatisfactory.

## Eligibility for Refunds
- You can request a refund if the item was never delivered.
- You can request a refund if the item received does not match the description or photos.
- Refunds must be requested within 14 days of the original delivery date.

## Refund Process
- To initiate a refund, navigate to "My Orders" and click "Dispute/Refund".
- The vendor has 48 hours to respond. If they fail to respond, Vendora automatically approves the refund.
- Refunds are processed back to the original payment method (JazzCash, Easypaisa, SadaPay, or Bank Transfer). Cash on Delivery (COD) refunds will be issued to your digital wallet.

## Timelines
- Approved refunds take 3-5 business days to reflect in your account.
