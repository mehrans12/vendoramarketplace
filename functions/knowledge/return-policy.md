# Vendora Return & Refund Policy

## General Return Policy
- Products can be returned within 7 days of delivery.
- Items must be unused, in their original packaging, with all tags attached.
- To initiate a return, buyers must contact support@vendora.pk with their order ID and a picture of the received item.

## Refunds
- Refunds are processed once the vendor receives the returned item and verifies its condition.
- For Cash on Delivery (COD) orders, refunds are credited to the user's Vendora Wallet or can be transferred to Easypaisa/JazzCash upon request.
- For prepaid orders (Easypaisa, JazzCash, SadaPay), the refund will be reversed to the original payment method within 5-7 business days.

## Exceptions
- Perishable goods (e.g., fresh food, custom baked items) cannot be returned.
- Personalized or custom-made items (e.g., custom engraved jewelry) are non-refundable unless they arrived damaged or defective.
